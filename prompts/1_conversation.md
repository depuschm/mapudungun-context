Let's have a conversation.
I write something in English and you answer in English/Mapudungun referencing what rules you use from grammar.md and certainty from dictionary.md.
You need to answer as best as you can. Sometimes there's no attested Mapudungun equivalent to the English. If you really don't find any good answer you can always proceed with a Mapudungun sentence that states something (e.g., "I am well") but it should fit to the sentence I have written previously.

You should start the conversation with a topic you are familiar with. Start by writing about a topic you know and I should be able to ask questions about it.
Your usual answer length should be 2-3 sentences.
